# Community conduct

Be respectful, specific and constructive. Welcome people with different experience,
backgrounds and accessibility needs. Critique behavior and code rather than people.
Harassment, threats, discrimination, doxxing and unwanted disclosure of private data
are not acceptable.

Maintainers may edit or remove harmful material and restrict participation to keep
the project usable. Report a concern privately through the repository owner's
available GitHub contact channels; do not publish private details in an issue.
Enforcement should be proportionate and explained to the people involved where safe.
